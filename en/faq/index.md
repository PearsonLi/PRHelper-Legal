<details class="faq-item" data-faq-id="pre-pr-credit"><summary>How does pre-PR credit work?</summary><p>Pre-PR credit only applies to citizenship and changes with the rolling evaluation window.</p></details>

<details class="faq-item" data-faq-id="capabilities"><summary>What can I manage in CanResidency?</summary><p>Organize verified trips, review PR and citizenship timelines, assess future-travel impact, export records, and maintain separate family timelines.</p></details>
