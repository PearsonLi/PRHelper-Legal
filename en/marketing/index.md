<h2>Replace the residency spreadsheet</h2>
<p>CanResidency is an independent iPhone and iPad app for Canadian permanent residents who travel. Record absences once, then use the same travel history to monitor PR residency-obligation progress, plan citizenship physical presence, and prepare exportable records.</p>

<div class="feature-grid">
  <div class="feature-card"><h3>PR residency obligation</h3><p>Monitor progress toward the 730-day requirement across a rolling five-year window.</p></div>
  <div class="feature-card"><h3>Citizenship planning</h3><p>Track progress toward 1,095 days of physical presence and account for eligible pre-PR credit.</p></div>
  <div class="feature-card"><h3>Travel before you book</h3><p>Simulate a future trip and see how the planned absence changes your timeline.</p></div>
  <div class="feature-card"><h3>One place for the family</h3><p>Maintain separate profiles and travel histories for as many as 10 family members.</p></div>
  <div class="feature-card"><h3>Exportable travel history</h3><p>Keep a structured record that is easier to review before PR-card or citizenship applications.</p></div>
  <div class="feature-card"><h3>Local-first privacy</h3><p>Records stay on your device, with optional iCloud backup for continuity.</p></div>
</div>
