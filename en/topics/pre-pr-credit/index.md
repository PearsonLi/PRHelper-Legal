<h2>Quick Take</h2>
<p>Pre-PR credit applies to citizenship only and is constrained by eligibility windows.</p>

<h2>Question 1: When is pre-PR credit counted?</h2>
<p>Only days inside the citizenship eligibility window can contribute credit.</p>

<h2>Question 2: Why can credit show as zero?</h2>
<p>The eligible window may be closed, or start date can be later than end date.</p>

<h2>Question 3: How should users verify?</h2>
<p>Adjust evaluation day and verify whether an eligible pre-PR window still exists.</p>
