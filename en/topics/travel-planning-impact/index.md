<h2>Quick Take</h2>
<p>Trip planning should include residency impact, not only schedule and ticket costs.</p>

<h2>Question 1: Why add plans before booking?</h2>
<p>It exposes risk before commitment, helping avoid timeline surprises later.</p>

<h2>Question 2: How to read signal lights?</h2>
<p>Green means safer margin, yellow means near boundary, red means clear risk.</p>

<h2>Question 3: How to reduce risk?</h2>
<p>Avoid long continuous absences, split travel blocks, and protect key in-Canada days.</p>
