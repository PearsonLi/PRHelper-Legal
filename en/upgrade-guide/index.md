<h2>Stay free when the basic record is enough</h2>
<p>Keep the free version if up to 10 trip records let you validate the workflow and you do not yet need advanced planning, exports or multiple family members.</p>

<h2>Choose by task, not by feature count</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Monthly:</strong> flexible access to Pro + Family during a renewal, citizenship-preparation period or travel-heavy season.</li>
  <li><strong>Pro Lifetime:</strong> permanent individual access for a long travel history, planning signals and exports.</li>
  <li><strong>Family Lifetime:</strong> permanent Pro access plus separate records for people whose travel and eligibility dates differ.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Three questions before paying</h2>
<ol><li>Have you entered enough real trips to trust the workflow?</li><li>Is the limitation blocking a task you need now?</li><li>Do you need individual or multi-member records?</li></ol>
