<h2>Stay free when the basic record is enough</h2>
<p>Keep the free version if up to 5 trip records let you validate the workflow and you do not yet need advanced planning, exports or multiple family members.</p>

<h2>Choose by task, not by feature count</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Monthly:</strong> flexible access to Pro + Family during a renewal, citizenship-preparation period or travel-heavy season.</li>
  <li><strong>Pro Lifetime:</strong> permanent individual access for a long travel history, planning signals and exports.</li>
  <li><strong>Family Lifetime:</strong> permanent Pro access plus separate records for people whose travel and eligibility dates differ.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Three questions before paying</h2>
<ol><li>Have you entered enough real trips to trust the workflow?</li><li>Is the limitation blocking a task you need now?</li><li>Do you need individual or multi-member records?</li></ol>

<h2>Define the first paid-session outcome</h2>
<p>Before upgrading, write down one outcome to complete immediately: add the 6th verified trip, test one future absence, export one review copy, or create one separate family record. Recheck that outcome within seven days; if it is still incomplete, use the support checklist instead of changing plans.</p>

<h2>Return to the plan only when the job changes</h2>
<p>If you stopped after the free test, come back when one of four signals appears: the 6th trip, a renewal or citizenship date you need to model, an export you need to keep, or a second family member with a different timeline. Reopen the smallest plan that removes that specific block; do not pay simply because time passed.</p>
