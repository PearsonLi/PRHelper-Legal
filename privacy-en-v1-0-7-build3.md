# CanResidency Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-03-29

Applicable App Version: v1.0.7 (build 3)

This Privacy Policy explains how CanResidency handles your data.

1. Scope  
   This policy applies to the mobile app CanResidency.

2. Data we process  
   Data may include records you provide or authorize, such as travel dates, destination country, purpose, notes, PR settings, and temporary residence records.

3. No in-app account creation  
   The app does not require in-app account registration or login. We do not maintain a user account database for this app.

4. Purpose of processing  
   Data is used to provide residency-day calculations, progress views, reminders, charts, and export features.

5. Storage and sync  
   Data is stored locally by default. If iCloud sync is enabled on your device/account, data may sync through Apple iCloud services. We do not upload this app data to our own servers.

6. Timezone basis  
   Day-level calculations use Canada timezone (America/Toronto) to keep consistent counting behavior.

7. Permissions  
   Notification permission is optional and is used for reminder features. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings at any time.

8. Export and sharing  
   Export is initiated by you. Exported files may contain personal travel details. You are responsible for where and how these files are shared.

9. Payment-related information  
   Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple. We receive purchase entitlement status needed to unlock features (including Pro and Family capability states), but not full payment card data.

10. Retention and deletion  
   You can modify or delete records in-app. Uninstalling the app removes local app data from the device. iCloud backup/sync data is managed through your Apple account settings.

11. Children  
   The app is not directed to children under 13.

12. Policy updates and contact  
   We may update this policy from time to time.  
   Contact: pearson@rhotechnologies.ca
