# Canada Residency Pro Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-03-08

This Privacy Policy explains how Canada Residency Pro processes travel and residency data on your device.

1. Data we process  
   This includes data you enter or authorize: paired departure/entry dates, destination country, purpose, notes, PR landing date, PR card expiry/reminder settings, temporary-residence records for citizenship credit, and import/export text/file content you explicitly provide.

2. Purpose of processing  
   Data is used to calculate PR/citizenship residency days under IRCC 5-year rolling rules, and to provide projection, risk reminders, trend charts, and travel-record import/export.

3. Timezone basis  
   All day-level records, date boundaries, and calculations use Canada timezone (America/Toronto), independent of device local timezone.

4. Storage and transfer  
   Data is stored locally by default and is not uploaded to developer servers; it is not used for ad profiling and is not sold.

5. Export and sharing  
   Export/sharing is user-initiated. Exported files may include dates, countries, purposes, and notes. Please review before sharing.

6. Payment-related data  
   Monthly subscription and one-time purchase are processed by Apple App Store. The app only receives necessary purchase status for entitlement (such as subscription validity), not full payment card details.

7. Backup and migration  
   If iCloud device backup is enabled, local app data may be included by the system and restored on a new device.

8. Your controls  
   You can edit/delete records or uninstall the app at any time. Uninstall removes local data (iCloud backups are managed by you in system settings).

9. Policy updates  
   This policy may change with app versions. The latest version is available inside the app.
