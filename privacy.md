# 枫叶卡管家 / Canada Residency Pro 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-02-27

## 中文

本隐私政策适用于“枫叶卡管家”在你的设备上对旅行记录与居住统计数据的处理。

1. 我们处理的数据  
   包括你录入或授权获取的数据：出入境日期（可含未闭环计划）、目标国家、目的、备注、永居登陆日、永居卡到期日与提醒设置，以及定位采样（时间戳、经纬度、国家/地区/城市、来源）。

2. 数据用途  
   用于按 IRCC 5 年滚动规则计算永居/入籍居住天数，并提供达标预测、情景问答、趋势图、旅行记录导出等功能。

3. 时区口径  
   所有按天记录、日期切分与统计均以加拿大时区（America/Toronto）为准，不随设备本地时区变化。

4. 存储与传输  
   数据默认仅保存在本机，不上传至开发者服务器；不用于广告画像，也不会出售你的行程数据。

5. 权限与定位  
   仅在你同意并开启后使用前台定位，采样频率约每小时 1 次；不使用持续后台定位。

6. 导出与分享  
   导出/分享由你主动触发。导出文件可能包含日期、国家、目的和备注，请在分享前自行确认。

7. 支付相关信息  
   订阅与买断由 Apple App Store 处理。应用仅接收必要的购买状态（如订阅有效期）用于授权，不接收银行卡等完整支付信息。

8. 备份与迁移  
   若启用 iCloud 设备备份，本地数据可能被系统纳入备份；换机可通过 iCloud 备份恢复。

9. 你的控制权  
   你可随时修改/删除记录、关闭定位、撤回权限或卸载应用。卸载会移除本机数据（iCloud 备份需你在系统中自行管理）。

10. 政策更新  
   本政策可能随版本更新，最新版本会在应用内展示。

## English

This Privacy Policy explains how Canada Residency Pro processes travel and residency data on your device.

1. Data we process  
   This includes data you enter or authorize: departure/entry dates (including open plans), destination country, purpose, notes, PR landing date, PR card expiry/reminder settings, and location samples (timestamp, coordinates, country/region/city, source).

2. Purpose of processing  
   Data is used to calculate PR/citizenship residency days under IRCC 5-year rolling rules, and to provide forecast, scenario Q&A, trend charts, and travel-record export.

3. Timezone basis  
   All day-level records, date boundaries, and calculations use Canada timezone (America/Toronto), independent of device local timezone.

4. Storage and transfer  
   Data is stored locally by default and is not uploaded to developer servers; it is not used for ad profiling and is not sold.

5. Permissions and location  
   Foreground location is used only after your consent, sampled about once per hour; no continuous background tracking is used.

6. Export and sharing  
   Export/sharing is user-initiated. Exported files may include dates, countries, purposes, and notes. Please review before sharing.

7. Payment-related data  
   Subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple App Store. The app only receives necessary purchase status for entitlement (such as subscription validity), not full payment card details.

8. Backup and migration  
   If iCloud device backup is enabled, local app data may be included by the system and restored on a new device.

9. Your controls  
   You can edit/delete records, disable location, revoke permission, or uninstall the app at any time. Uninstall removes local data (iCloud backups are managed by you in system settings).

10. Policy updates  
   This policy may change with app versions. The latest version is available inside the app.
