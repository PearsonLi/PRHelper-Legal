# What's New (v1.0.5 build 4)

## EN
Release: v1.0.5 (build 4)  
Compared with v1.0.4 (build 8):
- Added a new monthly-calendar mode to PR and Citizenship pages, and made Calendar Mode the default entry.
- Added Trend Mode and future evaluation controls with a fixed horizon of today through the next 3 years.
- Moved Temporary Residence management out of Citizenship into a collapsible section on Travel (below history), keeping full add/edit/delete support.
- Simplified information architecture: removed PR/Citizenship rule tabs and consolidated rule explanations into Settings → FAQ.
- Improved travel readability and compliance visuals: historical rows are cleaner, planned trips keep full PR/Citizenship signal indicators.
- Strengthened migration/recovery and free-tier boundaries to better preserve family data after reinstall/restore.

## ZH
发布版本：v1.0.5（build 4）  
相较于 v1.0.4（build 8）：
- 永居与入籍页面新增月历模式，并默认进入“日历模式”。
- 新增“趋势模式”，评估范围固定为“今天起未来 3 年”。
- 临时居留管理从入籍页迁移到旅行页历史记录下方的可折叠卡片，保留新增/编辑/删除完整能力。
- 页面结构简化：移除永居/入籍规则 Tab，统一收敛到“设置 → 常见问题”。
- 旅行页展示优化：历史记录去除右侧信号区，计划记录继续显示完整永居/入籍信号信息。
- 迁移与恢复路径、免费版边界进一步收口，重装/恢复时家庭数据保留更可靠。
