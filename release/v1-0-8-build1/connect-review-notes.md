# Connect Review Notes - v1.0.8 (build 1)

## EN

v1.0.8 (build 1)

- No login or app account is required. Core travel records, temporary residence records, and residency calculations run locally on device.
- Purchases are handled through Apple in-app purchase and RevenueCat entitlements. Pro Monthly unlocks Pro + Family while active; Pro Lifetime unlocks Pro; Family Lifetime unlocks Pro + Family multi-member features.
- Suggested review path: launch the app, add a Travel record, add a Temporary Residence record from Travel, switch to PR/Citizenship pages, then open Settings -> Unlock Pro to view purchase/restore options.
- This build specifically hardens local/iCloud persistence and migration paths so user travel, temporary residence, and family member records are preserved across relaunch, reinstall, and entitlement refresh.
- Legal links for this build: Terms https://pearsonli.github.io/PRHelper-Legal/terms/en/v1-0-8-build1/ and Privacy https://pearsonli.github.io/PRHelper-Legal/privacy/en/v1-0-8-build1/.

## ZH

v1.0.8 (build 1)

- 应用无需登录或创建应用内账号。旅行记录、临时居留记录和居住天数计算默认在本机完成。
- 购买由 Apple 内购与 RevenueCat 权益处理。Pro 月订阅在有效期内解锁 Pro + Family；Pro 买断解锁 Pro；Family 买断解锁 Pro + Family 多成员能力。
- 建议审核路径：启动应用，新增旅行记录，在旅行页新增临时居留记录，查看永居/入籍页面，然后进入设置 -> 解锁专业版查看购买与恢复入口。
- 本版本重点加强本地/iCloud 持久化与迁移路径，避免重启、重装或权益刷新后旅行、临时居留和家庭成员记录丢失。
- 本版本法律链接：服务条款 https://pearsonli.github.io/PRHelper-Legal/terms/zh/v1-0-8-build1/，隐私政策 https://pearsonli.github.io/PRHelper-Legal/privacy/zh/v1-0-8-build1/。
