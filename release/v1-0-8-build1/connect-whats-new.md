# Connect What's New - v1.0.8 (build 1)

- Previous commercial baseline: v1.0.7-build1

## EN

v1.0.8 (build 1)

- Improved data preservation for temporary residence records, trip history, and family member data after purchase, relaunch, reinstall, or iCloud restore.
- Fixed family member switching and member-scoped calculations so Overview, PR, Citizenship, and member labels stay aligned with the selected person.
- Strengthened local snapshot and iCloud restore handling to avoid empty restore overwrites and keep complete travel records available.

## ZH

v1.0.8 (build 1)

- 优化临时居留、旅行记录和家庭成员数据的保存逻辑，降低购买、重启、重装或 iCloud 恢复后数据丢失风险。
- 修复家庭成员切换与成员维度计算，让总览、永居、入籍页面和成员标签与当前选择成员保持一致。
- 加强本地快照与 iCloud 恢复保护，避免空数据覆盖，并尽量保留完整旅行记录。
